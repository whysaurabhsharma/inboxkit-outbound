# LinkedIn

Sent on days 1, 4 and 10 of the cadence in copy/sequence.md. Follow-ups
go only to accepted connections. No links in any message.

## Connection note (day 1)

Under 300 characters with merge fields filled. Names the signal, no
pitch, no link, asks only to connect.

{{first_name}}, you posted a {{signal_role}} role on {{signal_date}}. I work on mailbox and domain setup for outbound agencies and follow teams that are adding seats. Open to connecting?

## Follow-up 1 (day 4)

Gives something, asks nothing.

Thanks for connecting. A planning note for the {{signal_role}} hire: new mailboxes take 14 days of warmup to reach 40 sends a day (C07), so mailboxes warmed from the week the role is posted can carry volume 14 days later.

## Follow-up 2 (day 10)

The 30 minute ask, two lines.

If the new seat needs mailboxes, I can size them for its volume and price them two ways.
Worth 30 minutes to size and price the mailboxes?
