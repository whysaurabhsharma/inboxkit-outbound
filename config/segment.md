# Segment, signal, offer, CTA

Decisions made 25 Oct 2026. Each one is a choice, not an assumption.

## The bet

Outbound and lead generation agencies of 5 to 50 people that already
run Smartlead or Instantly and show a dated scale signal inside the
last 90 days, most often a live job post for an outbound, SDR or
deliverability role.

Infrastructure gets bought when sending volume is about to change.
A hiring post is the most public, dated, checkable proof that it is
about to. The techstack filter means InboxKit plugs into what they
already use, so the migration objection never comes up.

Same thesis proved at Dot: reach the buyer when a dated signal shows
the team is stretched. Applied here to a buyer who runs outbound for
a living.

## Signal scoring

| Signal | Dated | Maps to a purchase | Sourcable | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hiring outbound or SDR | Yes, dated URL | Strong | Apify, LinkedIn Jobs | Primary |
| Techstack users | Yes, posts and Clay | Strong | Yes | Filter |
| Job changers | Yes, start date | Medium | Clay | Secondary |
| Post engagers | Yes, post date | Medium | Apify | Hold |
| Competitor followers | Weak | Weak | Yes | Sourcing only |
| Recent funding | Yes | Weak for agencies | Yes | Skip |

## Qualified demo

An outbound or lead generation agency, booked with the person who
owns the infrastructure decision (founder, co-founder, Head of
Growth, Head of Outbound, Head of Deliverability, Ops lead), at a
stated or inferred cold email volume of 10k a month or higher.

10k is their own second band on the demo form, not a number I made
up. AEs and SDRs are excluded by title at the contact step.

## Offer

A 30 minute agency demo that sizes mailbox count for the volume
implied by their job post, prices it two ways, and shows warmup
running on one of their own domains.

Destination: https://www.inboxkit.com/demo

Pricing it two ways is the part that earns the meeting. For an
agency sizing past roughly 200 mailboxes, Azure tenants at $30 for
up to 100 mailboxes (C04) land near $0.30 a mailbox against $3.00
on the standard rate (C01). Most agency founders read the pricing
page, see the flat rate and stop there.

## CTA

One question, asking for the 30 minutes their own form books.
Every email ends with it or with nothing. The LinkedIn connection
note asks only to connect.

## Why it works

The purchase is volume triggered. The signal dates the volume
change. The integration removes the switching cost.
