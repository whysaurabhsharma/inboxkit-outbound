## Fit against the ICP

Neither qualifying agency can be confirmed inside the 5 to 50 size
band, because job boards do not publish headcount; `employee_count`
reads "Not found" for both. Both are well known agencies that appear
to be larger than 50 people, but that is not sourced, so the size
filter was not applied and no size points were scored. Neither job
post mentions Smartlead or Instantly, so the techstack reads
"Not found" too.

## What this says about small agencies

The run is consistent with small agencies not using ATS job boards:
14 of 22 named agencies had no board on any of the five platforms,
and 4 of the 6 approved boards had no open jobs.

It is weak evidence on its own. The probe list was agencies that
could be named from general knowledge, which skews towards the
larger, better known firms, and six boards is a small sample. If even
these firms mostly do not post on ATS boards, smaller ones are less
likely to, but the run did not test small agencies directly.

## Consequence for the 100 row sheet

The sheet is a hybrid dry run. `data/fixtures/companies.json` holds
the 2 live board rows (`data_source` = live_board) and 98 synthetic
rows (`data_source` = synthetic_dry_run) generated by
`data/fixtures/make_synthetic.py`. Synthetic companies use the
reserved .example domain so none can be a real company.
