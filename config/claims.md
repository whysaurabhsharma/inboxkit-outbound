## Conflicts found and how they were resolved

Three figures appear at two values on their own site. Each is
resolved down, which is one sentence in the memo.

1. Inbox placement. The agency page says 95 percent in two places
   and 90 percent plus in its own stat block. The homepage says
   90 percent plus. Resolved to 90 percent plus (C05).
2. Integrations. The FAQ says 24 plus, the comparison table says
   20 plus. Resolved to 20 plus (C06).
3. Setup time. The homepage feature grid says 20 minutes, a section
   header says 10 minutes, the FAQ says under 10 minutes, and the
   order FAQ says 30 to 120 minutes. The last is the most specific
   and the most conservative. Resolved to 30 to 120 minutes (C09).

## Qualified demo

Defined from their own demo form at https://www.inboxkit.com/demo

The form books a 30 minute call and asks one qualifying question:
target cold email volume, in four bands.

| Band | Qualified |
| --- | --- |
| Less than 10k per month | No |
| 10k to 100k per month | Yes |
| 100k to 500k per month | Yes |
| 500k plus per month | Yes |

A qualified demo is an outbound or lead generation agency, booked
with the person who owns the infrastructure decision (founder,
co-founder, Head of Growth, Head of Outbound, Head of
Deliverability, Ops lead), at a stated or inferred volume of 10k
a month or higher. AEs and SDRs are excluded by title at the
contact step.

The CTA asks for the 30 minutes their form already books.
